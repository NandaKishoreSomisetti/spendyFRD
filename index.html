About spendyFRD

Student Money Companion

spendyFRD is engineered specifically to help college students conquer their monthly allowance, hostel mess bills, UPI micro-transactions, and cash spends with complete clarity.

Instant Statement & Cash Clarity

Drag and drop PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, or bank statements (PDF, CSV, TXT) and log pocket cash to compute real net profit or loss instantly.

Smart Student Savings Advisor

Analyzes overspending on food delivery, late-night snacks, or quick-commerce and recommends achievable monthly savings using the 80/20 campus rule.

100% Private & Browser-Native

All parsing, calculations, and data persistence happen strictly in your browser via local storage. Your financial data never touches an external server.
